About Bacoor City

Know more about our rich history and vision for the future

Our History

Bacoor, officially known as the City of Bacoor, is a first-class component city in the province of Cavite, Philippines. According to the 2020 census, it has a population of 664,625 people, making it the most populous city in the province.

The city was created through Republic Act No. 9017, signed by President Gloria Macapagal Arroyo on March 15, 2002. However, Bacoor's history dates back to the Spanish colonial period, playing a significant role in the Philippine Revolution.

Today, Bacoor has transformed into a progressive city, balancing urban development with its rich cultural heritage. The city continues to honor its historical significance while embracing modern governance and sustainable development.

City Information

Geography & Climate

Location: Bacoor is located in the northeastern portion of Cavite province, bordered by Las Piñas and Parañaque to the north, Kawit to the south, Imus to the southwest, and Manila Bay to the east.

Land Area: 52.4 square kilometers

Climate: Bacoor has a tropical climate with two distinct seasons - the dry season from November to April and the wet season from May to October.

Barangays: The city is composed of 73 barangays, making it one of the most densely populated areas in the province.

Economic Profile

Bacoor's economy is diverse, with a mix of industrial, commercial, and service sectors. The city has experienced significant economic growth due to its strategic location near Metro Manila.

Key Industries:

  • Manufacturing and light industries
  • Retail and wholesale trade
  • Real estate and property development
  • Business process outsourcing (BPO)
  • Food and beverage services

The city continues to attract investors due to its accessibility, growing population, and supportive local government policies.

Culture & Heritage

Bacoor takes pride in its rich cultural heritage and historical significance in Philippine history.

Festivals:

  • Paruparo Festival - Celebrates the city's progress and transformation
  • Battle of Bacoor Anniversary - Commemorates the city's role in the Philippine Revolution

Historical Sites:

  • Bacoor Church (Immaculate Conception Parish)
  • Battle of Bacoor Monument
  • Heritage houses and ancestral homes
